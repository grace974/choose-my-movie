Refund Policy.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

We want you to feel confident purchasing Choose My Movie. This policy explains how refunds work for our one-time digital purchase.

1. Our Product

Choose My Movie is a digital web application. The current purchase price for lifetime access is $7.99 as displayed during checkout.

Because Choose My Movie is a digital service, there is no physical product to return.

2. Refund Window

30-Day Refund Window

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date.

To request a refund, contact us at support@choosemymovie.org using the email address associated with your purchase.

3. How to Request a Refund

Please email:

support@choosemymovie.org

Your request should include enough information for us to identify the purchase, such as:

  • The email address used for the purchase.
  • The approximate purchase date.
  • The name associated with the purchase, if applicable.
  • A brief description of the reason for your request, especially if you experienced a technical problem.

Please do not send complete credit card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive payment information by email.

4. Approved Refunds

If your refund request is approved, we will initiate the refund through the payment method or payment processor associated with the original transaction, where possible.

The time it takes for the refunded amount to appear in your account may depend on your bank, card issuer, or payment provider.

5. Technical Problems

If you paid for Choose My Movie but cannot access the service because of a technical or account-provisioning problem, please contact us before requesting a refund.

Our goal is to resolve legitimate access problems rather than leave you without the product you purchased.

We may troubleshoot issues such as account activation, login problems, missing purchase access, or other technical errors.

6. Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase Choose My Movie more than once for the same account, please contact us as soon as possible.

We may refund duplicate transactions when we can verify that the purchases were accidental duplicates.

7. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a purchase was made without your authorization, contact us promptly at support@choosemymovie.org .

We may request information needed to investigate the transaction.

If appropriate, you should also contact your bank or card issuer regarding an unauthorized transaction.

8. Refunds After the Refund Window

Refund requests submitted more than 30 days after the original purchase may not be eligible for a refund.

However, nothing in this policy is intended to limit any refund, cancellation, or consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded or waived under applicable law.

9. Service Changes or Discontinuation

Choose My Movie is a digital service and may change over time.

We may update features, interfaces, supported devices, integrations, or other parts of the service.

"Lifetime access" refers to access for the duration that the applicable service is offered and does not guarantee that the service will operate indefinitely or that every feature will remain unchanged.

If we permanently discontinue the service, any remedies or refunds will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances surrounding the discontinuation.

10. Chargebacks

If you believe there is a problem with a transaction, please contact us first so that we have an opportunity to resolve the issue.

Nothing in this section prevents you from exercising any rights you may have with your bank, card issuer, payment provider, or under applicable law.

11. No Subscription Charges

Choose My Movie currently uses a one-time $7.99 purchase for lifetime account access. There are no recurring monthly or annual subscription charges for the standard lifetime-access product.

If we introduce a different product or service with recurring charges in the future, the applicable pricing and billing terms will be displayed before purchase.

12. Contact

For refund questions or requests, contact:

Choose My Movie
support@choosemymovie.org

We will review refund requests and respond as soon as reasonably possible.

13. Changes to This Refund Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Any refund rights that apply to a purchase will be determined according to the policy and applicable law in effect at the relevant time, subject to any rights that cannot legally be limited.
