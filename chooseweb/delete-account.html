Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you no longer want to use Choose My Movie, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal information using the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Choose My Movie account, follow these steps:

  1. Open your email application.
  2. Send an email to support@choosemymovie.org.
  3. Use "Account Deletion Request" as the subject.
  4. Include the email address associated with your account so we can identify it.
Request Account Deletion

What Happens After Your Request?

Our support team will review your request and may contact you to verify account ownership before proceeding.

We will provide information about the next steps and any applicable processing timeframe after reviewing your request.

Important Information

  • Account deletion may be permanent and could remove account-related information.
  • Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate legal purposes.
  • Do not include your password or other sensitive login credentials in your deletion request.

Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion or need assistance, contact our support team.

Email: support@choosemymovie.org
