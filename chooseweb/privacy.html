Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Choose My Movie collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you visit our website or use our web application.

1. Who We Are

Choose My Movie is a digital movie discovery, tracking, watchlist, and recommendation service.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at:

Choose My Movie
Email: support@choosemymovie.org

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with Choose My Movie.

Information Examples Why We Collect It
Account information Email address, account identifier, authentication information To create and maintain your account and provide access to the app
Movie information Watchlist entries, watched movies, ratings, notes, reviews, genre tags To provide your personal movie tracking and recommendation features
Usage information Basic interactions, device/browser information, technical logs To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service
Payment information Transaction status and payment related identifiers To process purchases and verify access
Support information Emails and information you voluntarily provide when contacting support To respond to questions and provide customer support

3. Information You Provide

You may provide information directly to us when you:

  • Create or access a Choose My Movie account.
  • Purchase lifetime access.
  • Add movies to your watchlist.
  • Mark movies as watched.
  • Add ratings, reviews, notes, or genre information.
  • Contact customer support.
  • Submit information through forms on our website.

4. Movie Data You Add

Choose My Movie allows you to create personal movie tracking information, including watchlists, watched history, ratings, notes, reviews, and tags.

This information is associated with your account so that it can be synchronized across supported devices.

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information into movie reviews, notes, or other optional fields.

5. Automatically Collected Information

When you access our website or application, certain technical information may be collected automatically.

This may include:

  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type.
  • Operating system.
  • IP address or approximate network information.
  • Pages or application screens accessed.
  • Date and time of interactions.
  • Error and diagnostic information.

This information may be used to maintain security, diagnose technical problems, understand general usage, and improve the service.

6. How We Use Information

We may use information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate your account and provide access.
  • Provide movie tracking, watchlist, and recommendation features.
  • Synchronize your account across supported devices.
  • Process and verify purchases.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain and improve the website and application.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on where you live, we may process personal information under one or more legal bases, including:

  • Performing a contract with you, such as providing your account and purchased services.
  • Your consent, where consent is required.
  • Our legitimate interests, such as maintaining security and improving the service.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.

8. Payments

Payments for Choose My Movie may be processed by a third-party payment processor.

We may receive information such as transaction status, purchase amount, payment confirmation, and transaction identifiers.

We do not intentionally store complete credit or debit card numbers on our own application servers.

Payment information is handled according to the applicable payment provider's privacy policy and security practices.

9. Service Providers

We may use third-party companies to help operate Choose My Movie.

Depending on the services implemented, these providers may include companies providing:

  • Website and application hosting.
  • Database infrastructure.
  • Authentication and email delivery.
  • Payment processing.
  • Analytics and error monitoring.
  • Security and fraud prevention.

These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

10. Advertising and Analytics

Our marketing website may use analytics, advertising, or conversion-measurement technologies.

These technologies may help us understand website traffic, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and improve our marketing.

Depending on the technologies actually installed on the website, this may include cookies, pixels, tags, or similar technologies.

Before launch, this section should be updated to list the specific analytics and advertising providers actually installed on the website.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, maintain security, and understand website usage.

Some cookies may be necessary for the website to function, while others may be used for analytics or advertising.

Depending on your location, you may have choices regarding non-essential cookies through browser settings or a cookie consent mechanism.

12. Account Authentication

Choose My Movie may use email-based authentication, including magic links or similar passwordless login methods.

Your email address may be used to send authentication messages, account notices, security communications, and service-related information.

13. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information as a product.

We may share information with service providers and other parties when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide and maintain Choose My Movie.
  • Process payments.
  • Provide hosting, infrastructure, authentication, email, analytics, or security services.
  • Comply with legal requirements.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users, Choose My Movie, or others.
  • Facilitate a business transaction such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

14. Third-Party Streaming Platforms

Choose My Movie may reference services such as Netflix, Hulu, Max, Disney+, Prime Video, and Apple TV.

These companies are separate from Choose My Movie.

Choose My Movie is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected with those services unless expressly stated.

Their respective names, trademarks, and logos belong to their respective owners.

15. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

16. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Choose My Movie account.

If the application provides a "Delete Account" feature, you may use that feature to submit your request.

You may also contact us at support@choosemymovie.org for assistance.

Certain information may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, accounting, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal purposes.

17. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These rights may include the ability to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information.
  • Request a copy or portability of certain information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Exercise other rights provided by applicable law.

To make a privacy request, contact:

support@choosemymovie.org

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

18. Children's Privacy

Choose My Movie is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where doing so would violate applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us at support@choosemymovie.org .

19. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

20. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required by applicable law, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

21. Third-Party Links

Our website or application may contain links to third-party websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

22. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

If changes are material, we may provide additional notice where required by applicable law.

23. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at:

Choose My Movie
